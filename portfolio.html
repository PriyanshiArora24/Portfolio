Organic growth in ~7 months
During the COVID lockdown in 2020, I started a fan page out of pure curiosity to learn image and video editing. It grew to 11K+ followers in around 7 months, purely through regular posting and finding a rhythm with content. There was no strategy behind it, just consistency and a genuine interest in creating. I stopped posting after 2022 as studies got demanding, and the account has since gone inactive.

That experience gave me a real feel for what content connects with people, what keeps an audience coming back, and how small but consistent effort compounds over time.
